# Seven lines for anyone starting something new

Ralph Waldo Emerson told us: “Do not follow where the path may lead. Go instead where there is no path and leave a trail.”

Albert Einstein supposedly said that "insanity is doing the same thing over and over again and expecting different results."

Hamlet asks the oldest question of all: “To be, or not to be, that is the question.”

As Mark Twain put it, “The only thing we have to fear is fear itself.”

Peter Drucker said it best: “The best way to predict the future is to create it.”

> Every new beginning comes from some other beginning’s end.
> — Seneca

And one from my own notebook: “The quiet hours before dawn belong to people who keep their own promises.”
